The Map, zoomed to the path.

**Already on 1.4.0?** PingerPlot offers this update by itself (or ⋯ → **Check for updates**): click **Install and restart**. Otherwise: [`PingerPlot-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/PingerPlot/releases/download/v1.4.1/PingerPlot-Setup.exe) (installs for your Windows user, no admin prompt) or [`PingerPlot-win64.zip`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/PingerPlot/releases/download/v1.4.1/PingerPlot-win64.zip) (portable). Not code-signed: *Windows protected your PC* → **More info → Run anyway**, and compare with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### Changed

- **The Map zooms to the path.** A path within one region used to sit in a clump of a few pixels on a world map; it now fills the view, without stretching. With nothing located yet it's still the whole world.
- Map labels step off the path's line where they can and stay on the map; one that has to cross the line sits on a plate so it stays readable.

The full list is in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/jamesccupps/PingerPlot/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
