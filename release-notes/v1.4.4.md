Signed downloads: the installer, the uninstaller and both programs are code-signed by James Cupps. No other changes from 1.4.3.

If Windows shows *Windows protected your PC*, **More info** names the publisher, James Cupps; `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's hash.

### Security

- **Code-signed** (Azure Artifact Signing, timestamped): `PingerPlot.exe` and `pingerplot-headless.exe` in both downloads, `PingerPlot-Setup.exe` and the uninstaller. Windows shows the publisher in the file's Properties → Digital Signatures and in its prompts.
- The release workflow signs in a job of its own that runs nothing from PyPI and never runs the program, then tests the signed files, every signature included, before the update manifest is signed and anything is published.
